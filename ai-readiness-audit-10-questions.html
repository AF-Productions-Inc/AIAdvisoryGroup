The AI Readiness Audit: 10 Questions to Ask Before Deploying AI Agents in Your Business

May 20, 2026 · 10 min read · AI Advisory Group

AI agent deployment has a high failure rate — not because the technology is unreliable, but because most businesses skip the readiness work that determines whether an implementation will succeed or stall. Work through these ten questions honestly before investing in any AI system.

1. Can you clearly describe the workflow you want to automate? Not in general terms — in specific operational detail. What is the trigger? What happens next? What decisions are made and by whom? If you cannot describe a workflow in detail, you cannot build an agent to execute it.

2. Do you have documented processes? AI agents are trained on information. If your business operates on tribal knowledge that exists only in the heads of specific team members, you have a documentation problem that must be solved first.

3. Is your data clean and accessible? If your customer records live in three different places and your pricing is on a whiteboard, AI integration will be complicated and expensive.

4. Who will own the AI system after implementation? AI agents require ongoing monitoring, periodic retraining, and a designated owner who understands the system well enough to identify when something is not working.

5. What does success look like? Define specific, measurable outcomes before deployment: response time, conversion rate, review velocity, hours saved. Measure before implementation to establish a baseline.

6. Are your team members bought in? Team members who feel threatened by automation either underuse the systems or actively work around them. Framing AI agents as tools that take repetitive work off their plates is essential.

7. Is your technology infrastructure compatible? AI agents need to connect to your existing tools. Identify what integrations are required before beginning the build.

8. What is your plan if the agent makes a mistake? Have human review mechanisms for high-stakes interactions and a clear escalation path when the agent cannot handle something well.

9. Do you have a realistic timeline? Effective AI agent deployment typically takes four to eight weeks for discovery, build, training, testing, and refinement.

10. Are you solving a real problem or chasing a trend? AI agent deployment should solve a specific, painful operational problem with a measurable cost. If the primary driver is wanting to say you are using AI, the implementation will not have clear enough success criteria to work.

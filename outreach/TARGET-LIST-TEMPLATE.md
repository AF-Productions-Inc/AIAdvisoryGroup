## SOURCING CRITERIA (who makes the list)
✅ 10–200 Google reviews (established but not enterprise — big enough to afford you, small enough to have the problem)
✅ No chat widget on website, or a form that goes unanswered after hours (test it)
✅ Owner-operated (CSLB lookup shows individual, not corporate franchise)
✅ Active — reviews within last 6 months (dead companies waste touches)
❌ Skip: franchises (decision is corporate), 1-truck solos (can't afford), companies with polished automated response already

## DAILY RHYTHM (fits 1–2 hr/day)
- 20 min: 5 personalized sends (email or SMS per cadence schedule)
- 10 min: log touches, move stages, set next actions
- Rest of time: audit calls when booked (these outrank everything — cancel other work for a booked audit)

# The 30-Minute AI Opportunity Audit — Call Script
**Goal:** Diagnose → Demo → Recommend a tier → Book the follow-up or close.
**Positioning:** You are the doctor, not the salesman. The audit IS valuable even if they never buy.

---

## PRE-CALL PREP (10 min, do the night before)
Pull up and note:
- [ ] Their website — does it have a contact form? Chat? What happens when you submit it? (Actually submit one at 8pm and time the response — this becomes your killer stat)
- [ ] Their Google Business Profile — review count, avg rating, date of last review, do they respond to reviews?
- [ ] 1–2 named competitors in their city with better review velocity
- [ ] Their service list + any pricing visible
- [ ] Guess at ticket size (HVAC install $8-15K, service call $200-600, roof $10-30K, fence $3-8K)

---

## MINUTES 0–3: FRAME THE CALL
"Hey [Name], thanks for making time. Here's how I like to run these 30 minutes so it's actually useful for you:

First 10 minutes — I ask about how leads and jobs flow through your business today. Middle 10 — I show you what I found when I looked at [Company] from the outside, plus a live demo of what an AI agent actually does. Last 10 — if it makes sense, I'll tell you exactly what I'd build for you and what it costs. And if it doesn't make sense, I'll tell you that too. Fair?"

(Getting a "yes" here = micro-commitment. Sets you as the one running the meeting.)

## MINUTES 3–12: DIAGNOSE (ask, then shut up and listen)
Core questions — pick 5-6, don't interrogate:

1. "Walk me through what happens when a lead comes in — from first contact to booked job."
2. "What happens to calls after 6pm or on Sundays?" ← almost always the wound
3. "Roughly how many inquiries a month? And how many turn into booked jobs?"
4. "Who handles the phones/messages now? What does that cost you?" (person, hours, salary)
5. "How do you ask for reviews today? Is anyone doing it every time?"
6. "Peak season — what breaks first?"
7. "What's an average job worth? What's a big one?"

**Listen for the money quote** — they'll say something like "yeah, we probably lose a few every week." WRITE IT DOWN verbatim. You'll repeat it back in minute 25.

## MINUTES 12–15: THE MIRROR (your outside findings)
"Before this call I did a little homework. Two things I found:

One — I submitted a test inquiry through your website at [8pm Tuesday]. As of this morning [it hadn't been answered / it took X hours]. Your competitor [Competitor] responded to the same test in [X minutes / also failed — opportunity either way].

Two — [Competitor] has added [X] Google reviews in the last 90 days. You've added [Y]. Same quality of work, I'm sure — but homeowners can't see quality. They can see reviews."

(This is the moment the call turns. You're no longer a vendor — you're the person who told them the truth.)

## MINUTES 15–22: THE LIVE DEMO (the close weapon)
"Let me show you what fixing this looks like. This is a live agent — not slides, not a video. YOU be the customer. Type whatever a real customer would type."

Hand them the demo URL / share screen. Coach them:
- "Try 'my AC is blowing warm air'"
- Watch it: greet → qualify (location, urgency, system age) → quote service call fee → offer appointment slots → book

Narrate the receipt: "That took 45 seconds. It's 9pm. Nobody at your office touched it. The job is on the calendar with the customer's info attached. Now imagine that on every inquiry, every night, all season."

**Pause. Let them react.** The reaction you're waiting for: "Can it do [my specific thing]?" — that's buying temperature. Answer honestly; "yes, we train it on YOUR services, YOUR pricing, YOUR service area" — pivot to close.

## MINUTES 22–28: THE PRESCRIPTION
"Based on what you told me — [repeat their money quote back] — here's what I'd actually build for you:"

**If small op / first AI / main pain is missed leads →**
"The Essential build: $5,497 one-time. Lead Response Agent + Review & Reputation Agent. Fixes both problems we found today, live in about 3 weeks, then $8K/month for me to run, tune, and report on it."

**If established / multi-crew / growth mode →**
"The Growth build: $9,997. Everything in Essential plus a lead generation engine that goes and FINDS work, and full customer-service automation. $10K/month managed."

**Anchor with the math:** "You told me an average job is $[X] and you're losing maybe [N] a week. That's $[monthly loss]. The system costs less than what you're currently losing — it pays for itself even if it only catches half."

## MINUTES 28–30: THE CLOSE (choose one path)
**Direct close (if hot):** "Want me to send the agreement today? Build starts this week, you're live before [season/month]."

**Scheduled close (if warm):** "Here's what I'll do — I'll write up everything we covered today: what I found, what I'd build, exact pricing. You'll have it by tomorrow morning. Let's put 15 minutes on the calendar for [day] to decide. Sound good?" ← Book it BEFORE hanging up.

**Honest no (if bad fit):** "Truthfully, I don't think you need us yet. Do [one free tactical fix] first. When you're at [threshold], call me." ← This builds referrals. Say it when true.

---

## OBJECTION RESPONSES (memorize these five)

**"It's too expensive."**
"Compared to what? A part-time office admin runs $2,500/month and doesn't work nights. You told me you're losing roughly $[X]/month in missed jobs. This is the cheapest employee you'll ever hire — and I'm accountable for its performance every month."

**"My customers want to talk to a real person."**
"They want a fast, helpful answer. At 9pm the choice isn't AI vs. human — it's AI vs. voicemail. And every complex or emotional call still routes to your team, with the customer's info already captured."

**"I need to think about it."**
"Totally fair. What specifically — the price, the tech, or whether it'll work for your business? [Answer that one thing.] Let's grab 15 minutes [day] and make the call either way."

**"We tried a chatbot before, it was garbage."**
"You're right, most are. A $50/month website chatbot with canned answers IS garbage. This is trained on your services, your pricing, your area, and it books real appointments into your real calendar. That's why the demo — you saw it, not a promise of it."

**"Can I just do this myself with ChatGPT?"**
"The chat part, kind of. The part that qualifies leads against your rules, connects to your calendar and dispatch software, texts review requests after each job, and gets tuned every month based on real performance — that's the build. That's what you're paying for. And honestly, your hours are worth more running the business."

- [ ] Log in pipeline tracker: stage, notes, money quote, objections

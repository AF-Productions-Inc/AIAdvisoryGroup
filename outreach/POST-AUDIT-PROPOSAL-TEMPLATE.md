# Post-Audit Proposal Email Template
Send within 4 hours of the audit call. Claude drafts this from your call notes in ~2 minutes.

---

**Subject:** [Company] — your AI audit findings + my recommendation

Hi [FirstName],

Great talking today. As promised, here's everything in writing — short and straight.

**What I found:**
1. [Finding 1 — e.g., "Your website inquiry from Tuesday 8pm went unanswered for 14 hours. Homeowners book with whoever responds first — that lead almost certainly called someone else."]
2. [Finding 2 — e.g., "You've added 4 Google reviews in 90 days; [Competitor] added 22. In local search, that gap decides who gets the call."]
3. [Their money quote — e.g., "You said it yourself: 'we probably lose a few every week.' At your average ticket of $[X], that's roughly $[Y]/month walking to competitors."]

**What I'd build for [Company]:**
The **[Essential/Growth] Build — $[5,497/9,997] one-time**, live in ~3 weeks:
- [Agent 1 — what it does for THEM specifically]
- [Agent 2 — what it does for THEM specifically]
- Trained on your services, pricing, service area, and booking calendar

Then **$[8,000/10,000]/month** for me to run it: monitoring, tuning, monthly ROI report showing exactly what it captured, and a direct line to me.

**The math:** System cost vs. your estimated $[Y]/month in missed jobs — it pays for itself catching [fraction] of what's currently slipping.

**Next step:** Reply "let's go" and I'll send the agreement + intake questionnaire today. Build starts this week; you're live before [seasonal hook / date].

Or if you'd rather talk first: [cal.com/aiadvisorygroup/30min]

— Abraham
AI Advisory Group · Irvine, CA

## Follow-up if no reply:
- **Day 2:** "Any questions on the proposal? Happy to jump on 10 minutes." 
- **Day 5:** One new piece of value (a stat, a competitor observation) + re-ask.
- **Day 10:** Breakup: "I'll assume timing isn't right — I'll check back before [season]. The findings in that email are yours to use either way."  → tag Seasonal Re-entry.
